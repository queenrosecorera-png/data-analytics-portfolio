# Social Media Trend Intelligence

An end-to-end Social Media Trend Intelligence project using Python and Power BI to analyze sentiment, engagement, trending topics, platforms, and user behavior. The project covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI development, visualization, and interactive dashboard creation.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sentiment distribution across social media posts.
- Identify the top-performing platform by views and engagement.
- Determine which content topics and account types drive the most engagement.
- Track views over time to identify trend patterns.
- Surface top trending keywords and hashtags for content strategy.

## Key Insights
- Sentiment was majority-positive across posts, with fewer negative and neutral posts.
- TikTok was the top-performing platform, both by total views (88M) and by engagement (7.2M).
- Climate & Environment content drove the highest topic-wise views (~35M), ahead of Entertainment and Education.
- Verified/Celebrity accounts generated dramatically higher engagement (16.7M) than Brand Accounts, Micro-Influencers, and Regular Users combined.
- Views over time fluctuated notably, peaking around May (~25M) and dipping in November (~14M) before rising again in December.
- Top trending keywords centered on "yoga, wellness tips," with #StudentLife and #OnlineLearning leading the top 10 hashtags.

## Technical Details
| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Tools Used | Python (Google Colab), Power BI |
| Dataset Size | 12,001 posts, 228M total views, 19M total likes, 2M total shares, 6 platforms |
| Visual Types | KPI cards, bar charts, stacked bar chart, line chart, donut/bar breakdowns, filters (platform, topic, sentiment, region, date) |
| Purpose | Identify content and platform trends to guide social media strategy |

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data cleaning and analysis of large-scale social media data using Python
- Sentiment and engagement analysis across platforms and content types
- Time-series trend analysis (views over time)
- Interactive dashboard design with multi-filter Power BI reports

## Business Impact
This analysis can help social media and marketing teams:
- Prioritize platforms and content topics with the highest engagement (e.g. TikTok, Climate & Environment)
- Partner with high-engagement account types like verified/celebrity creators
- Time content releases around seasonal engagement peaks
- Track trending keywords/hashtags to inform content planning
