# Startup Investment Analytics Dashboard (2020-2025)

An end-to-end Startup Investment Analytics project using Python, Power BI, and Looker Studio to analyze funding trends across industries, investment stages, cities, and investors. The project covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI development, visualization, and interactive dashboard creation.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze investment trends from 2020-2025 to identify growth and decline periods.
- Identify top-funded industries and funding stages driving the most capital.
- Compare city-wise investment activity to find emerging tech hubs.
- Identify leading investors by total capital deployed.

## Key Insights
- Investment activity was cyclical, peaking near $6B in both 2021 and 2024 before dropping sharply to $3.6B in 2025.
- FoodTech was the top-funded industry ($3.14B) among all sectors analyzed.
- Series C was the leading funding stage, accounting for ~$9.4B in investment.
- Pune led all cities by total investment (~$4.6B), surpassing larger tech hubs like Bengaluru and Mumbai.
- Accel was the top investor overall, with ~$1.36B deployed.
