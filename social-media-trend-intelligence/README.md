# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An analysis of a 5,000-record e-commerce sales dataset (Kaggle), tracking 5K orders totaling 5.11M in revenue, built using Python and Power BI.

## Business Objectives
- Identify top-performing product categories by revenue and units sold.
- Analyze payment method trends driving the most revenue.
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts, delivery speed, and customer ratings.
- Track revenue trends by region, weekday, and month.
- Build an interactive dashboard for ongoing sales tracking.

## Key Insights
- Electronics was the top-performing category by revenue (~1.8M) and made up 55.1% of total units sold (20K total units).
- Card payments generated the most revenue (2.4M), well ahead of COD (1.6M) and Wallet (1.0M).
- Revenue declined as the discount percentage increased, with 0-10% discount orders generating the most revenue.
- Customer ratings stayed consistently low (2.9-3.2 average) across all delivery-day buckets, showing no clear link between faster delivery and higher ratings.
- Revenue was fairly evenly distributed across all four regions (West, North, South, East) and across weekdays.
- Average delivery time was 6.12 days, with an average customer rating of 2.97.

## Business Impact
This analysis can help e-commerce teams:
- Focus inventory and marketing on top-performing categories like Electronics
- Reassess discount strategy given its negative impact on revenue
- Investigate satisfaction drivers beyond delivery speed, since ratings stay low regardless
- Identify and retain top revenue-driving customers
